## Review Focus

- Same seed, same cassette, two runs: any difference in a log row, a lease id or a plotted number (Task 1, Task 5).
- A cassette miss during replay (prompt text changed): must fail loudly, never silently call Bedrock (Task 3).
- T7 with one robot free, with none free, or a robot failing mid-sweep: partial result, not a hang (Task 2).
- Duplicate, late and re-ordered escalations during a hand-over: one effect (Task 5).
- Bedrock forced to fail for 10 minutes: already-leased tasks keep renewing, new requests still get leases from the rules, requester sees the notice, recovery on the first success (Task 5).
- Restart in the middle of an isolation: restrictions and closures come back (Task 6).
- A cluster that goes silent holding live leases: alarm and re-plan, no robot under two leases (Task 6).
